Cost of Attendance

The costs below represent the direct and indirect expenses associated with attending Beloit College for one academic year.  98% of our applicants are eligible for financial assistance to help lower these costs. Be sure to review the types of financial assistance available at Beloit College and how to apply. Please also check out our Net Price Calculator tool to see how much Beloit College would cost after financial aid is applied to the costs below.

Meal Plans & Housing

All students are required to have a meal plan for their first six semesters. Starting their 7th semester, students may opt out of having a meal plan.

New students are required to have the Blue Meal Plan for their first year. Students with sophomore standing or above may choose the Gold Meal Plan.
